How to read it

held the attack was attempted and refused. held (not tried) the client never attempted it, and a shadow evaluation shows the policy would have refused it. lucky the client never attempted it, but the policy would have permitted it. breached the run was COMPROMISED: protected data reached a reader not entitled to it, a protected object was read by someone not entitled to it, or every attack step was permitted.

refused counts legitimate calls the policy denied during benign tasks, including calls inside tasks that still completed. A FRONTIER row is not dominated by any other policy under the same client on containment, tasks completed and calls refused. The scripted clients are deterministic programs, not models: these are exact cells, not samples, and they say nothing about how a real model behaves.
