# Why Apache-2.0

MIT would also be fine for the code. Apache-2.0 was chosen for the explicit
patent grant (section 3), because the people this project most needs to adopt
it -- authorization-engine maintainers and security vendors -- have legal teams
that care about that clause, and because permissive licensing is the norm in
this category (AgentDojo, PIArena, DoomArena and inspect_evals are all MIT or
Apache).

Note for anyone tempted to borrow from adjacent work: ContainmentBench is
published CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. That is no-derivatives. Do not port its trace
schema or scenario specifications.
